Safety Rules

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Protect your identity, trust your boundaries, and report serious concerns.

1. Before Entering

  • Use a unique alias and password.
  • Hide mail, photos, windows, addresses, uniforms, and location clues.
  • Cover identifiable tattoos or objects if anonymity matters.
  • Do not show children, identification, financial documents, medication labels, or license plates.
  • Set your boundaries before joining a room.

2. During an Interaction

  • Trust discomfort and leave immediately; you do not owe an explanation.
  • Never share passwords, verification codes, payment credentials, or home address.
  • Do not send money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or financial assistance to another user.
  • Do not move to another app because of pressure or promises.
  • Use mute, block, exit, and report controls at the first sign of coercion or threats.

3. No Recording

Recording, screenshots, screen capture, rebroadcasting, and distribution without express permission are prohibited. Technical detection cannot guarantee prevention, so only reveal what you can safely risk being remembered or captured.

4. Suspected Minor or Exploitation

End the interaction immediately. Do not confront, investigate, download, forward, or redistribute suspected illegal content. Preserve only the information needed to make a lawful report and contact the platform.

5. Threats and Emergencies

Blurred Encounters is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For threats, stalking, blackmail, trafficking, or image-based abuse, preserve lawful evidence, secure your accounts, and seek help from appropriate local authorities or victim-support services.

6. Relationship Safety

Users are responsible for their own relationship agreements. Consider discussing boundaries with a partner when safe to do so. The platform cannot define cheating, guarantee secrecy, or prevent relationship consequences.

7. Contact

Safety reports: blurredencounters69@gmail.com

Include the alias, time, room, concern, and whether anyone is in immediate danger.
