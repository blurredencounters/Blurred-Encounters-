Payments and Refunds Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Payments are not active yet; these rules apply when checkout is enabled.

1. Current Status

Payments are not currently active on the public GitHub Pages prototype. Displayed plan names and prices are part of a market-interest test and do not by themselves create a purchase, membership, or entitlement.

2. Before Payments Launch

Before a charge is accepted, checkout must clearly show the seller, plan, price, billing frequency, taxes or fees, included features, renewal terms, cancellation method, and any material restrictions.

3. Recurring Memberships

When activated, a recurring plan will renew only after the user receives clear notice and provides affirmative authorization. The payment method may be charged each billing period until cancellation. A confirmation and receipt should be provided.

4. Cancellation

Users will be able to cancel future renewals through the payment provider, account settings, or by contacting support. Cancellation stops future billing and normally leaves access active through the paid period unless otherwise stated.

5. Refunds

Unless required by law or stated at checkout, digital membership fees are generally not refundable after paid access begins. Refunds may be considered for duplicate charges, verified unauthorized transactions, a material billing error, or a prolonged failure to provide the purchased service.

A refund request should be made promptly and include the account email, charge date, amount, plan, and reason. Approval is not guaranteed.

6. Trials and Promotions

Any free trial, discount, or promotional price must state its duration, eligibility, conversion price, and whether it automatically becomes a paid plan. One offer may be limited per person, household, device, or payment method.

7. Failed Payments and Chargebacks

Failed or reversed payments may cause access to be paused. Users should contact support before filing a chargeback when possible. Fraudulent use of stolen payment methods or abusive disputes may result in termination, but users retain rights provided by law.

8. Price Changes

Prices and included features may change. Existing recurring members should receive advance notice of material price changes where required and an opportunity to cancel before the new price is charged.
