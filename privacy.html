Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This starter policy must remain accurate as the product and data practices change.

1. Scope and Current Website Status

This Policy explains how Blurred Encounters handles personal information. The current GitHub Pages version is a public testing and waitlist website. It does not currently provide member accounts, live chat, blurred video, or payment processing.

The current waitlist form opens your device's email application. Information is sent only when you choose to press Send. As features are added, this Policy must be updated before those features launch.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Alias, email address, age confirmation, country or general region, and preferences.
  • Account, profile, membership, and authentication information when accounts launch.
  • Messages, reports, support requests, feedback, and moderation evidence.
  • Device, browser, IP address, timestamps, security logs, and approximate location.
  • Age-verification result and limited verification metadata; unnecessary identity images should not be retained.
  • Payment status, plan, receipt, and transaction identifiers from a payment provider; we should not store full card numbers.

3. How Information May Be Used

  • Operate accounts, waitlists, lounges, matching, messaging, voice, and blurred video.
  • Verify age and eligibility.
  • Process memberships and provide receipts.
  • Protect users, prevent fraud, enforce policies, and investigate reports.
  • Respond to support, legal requests, and safety concerns.
  • Measure website use and improve features.
  • Send service messages and marketing only where permitted and with available opt-out choices.

4. How Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared with hosting, analytics, email, payment, age-verification, security, moderation, and communications providers that need it to perform services. It may also be disclosed to comply with law, protect a person from harm, investigate fraud or abuse, enforce policies, or complete a business transfer.

Blurred Encounters does not sell personal information for money. This statement must be revisited if advertising or data-sharing practices change.

5. Age-Verification Data

Age-verification information should be used only to determine eligibility, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and document the verification result. Collection should be limited to what is necessary, access restricted, and raw identity material deleted as soon as reasonably possible unless retention is legally required.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The site may use necessary browser storage for the age gate and preferences. Analytics may be added to count visits and understand clicks. Before non-essential analytics or advertising tools are activated, appropriate notice and consent controls should be added where required.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, account operation, safety, fraud prevention, disputes, legal obligations, and enforcement. Retention periods may differ by data type. Deleted accounts may leave limited security, payment, report, and legal records.

8. Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards should be used, including access controls, encryption where appropriate, secure providers, logging, and incident response. No service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You may request access, correction, deletion, or information about your data by emailing blurredencounters69@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request and may retain information when permitted or required by law.

You can opt out of promotional email using the unsubscribe method provided. Service, security, payment, and legal messages may still be sent.

10. Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has provided personal information or accessed adult areas, we may restrict access, delete information where appropriate, preserve safety evidence, and make reports when required.

11. International Users

Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where providers operate. Additional privacy rights may apply depending on location. The service should not launch in a jurisdiction until its age, privacy, consumer, and content requirements have been evaluated.

12. Changes and Contact

This Policy may change as the product changes. The updated date will appear above. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to blurredencounters69@gmail.com.
